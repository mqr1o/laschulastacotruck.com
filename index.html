Our Story

BORN FROM THE STREETS

Las Chulas was born from a deep love for authentic Mexican street food — the kind your abuela made on Sunday mornings, filled with warmth and tradition.

Every tortilla is pressed fresh. Every marinade follows family recipes passed down through generations. Find us at 32575 Mission Blvd in Hayward.

We're not just serving tacos. We're sharing culture, community, and the kind of food that creates memories.
